What We Do

Building Tomorrow's Transportation

At Ogopogo Solar, we design, build, and race solar-powered vehicles that demonstrate the potential of renewable energy technology. Our interdisciplinary team works together to overcome engineering challenges and push the boundaries of sustainable transportation.

  • Custom solar array design and optimization
  • Advanced aerodynamic body development
  • Energy-efficient electrical systems
  • Lightweight composite materials research
