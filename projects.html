Our Innovations

Beyond building complete vehicles, our team develops specific technologies that advance solar mobility. Here are some of our key innovations:

Advanced Solar Array

Custom arrangement of high-efficiency cells that optimizes power generation across different angles and light conditions.

Battery Management

Proprietary system that maximizes battery life and performance while ensuring safe operation in racing conditions.

Telemetry Platform

Real-time data collection and analysis system providing crucial performance metrics to our race strategy team.

Aerodynamic Design

Computer-optimized shapes that minimize drag while maintaining stability across various weather conditions.
