Available Departments

Our team is organized into specialized departments, each playing a critical role in the design, construction, and operation of our solar vehicles.

Mechanical Department

The Mechanical Department is responsible for the structural design and analysis of the vehicle, including chassis development, suspension systems, aerodynamic optimization, and mechanical integration of components.

Available Team:

  • Vehicle Dynamics Team
  • Chassis Team
  • Aeroshell Team

Ongoing Projects:

  • Carbon fiber monocoque chassis development
  • Suspension optimization for efficiency
  • Aerodynamic body design and testing

Electrical & Software Department

The Electrical and Software Department designs and implements all electrical systems in our solar car, including power generation, energy storage, motor control, and vehicle telemetry systems. develops the telemetry, data acquisition, and control systems that monitor and manage the vehicle's performance in real-time during races and testing.

Available team:

  • Solar Array Team
  • Battery Team
  • Power electronics
  • Hardware (Low Voltage) subdivision
  • Software team

Ongoing Projects:

  • Maximum power point tracking (MPPT) optimization
  • Battery management system development
  • Motor controller efficiency improvements

Business Department

The Business Department manages team finances, sponsorships, public relations, and logistics for events and travel, ensuring the team has the resources needed to succeed.

Available team:

  • Marketing Team
  • Sponsorship Team
  • Finance Team

Ongoing Projects:

  • Corporate sponsorship development
  • Social media presence expansion
  • Community outreach programs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience to join?

No, prior experience is not required for most positions. We value enthusiasm and willingness to learn. New members typically receive training and mentorship from experienced team members.

How much time commitment is expected?

We typically expect members to commit 2-8 hours per week during the academic year. Time commitments may increase during competition preparation or specific project deadlines. We understand academic priorities and offer flexible scheduling.

Can I join if I'm not an engineering student?

Absolutely! We welcome students from all disciplines. Business, marketing, communications, design, and many other non-engineering backgrounds are valuable to our team. Our Business Department specifically seeks diverse academic backgrounds.

When can I apply?

We accept applications year-round, but our main recruitment periods are at the beginning of each semester (September and January). Early applications are encouraged as positions can fill quickly.

Questions? Get in Touch!

Still have questions about joining our team? Reach out to us directly and we'll be happy to help.
